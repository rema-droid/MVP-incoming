## 2026-07-31 - [Adding Descriptive Accessible Names to Icon-Only Action Buttons in Feeds & Overlays]
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons (such as Share, Close, and Like/Heart buttons) often lack screen-reader-accessible names when they contain only decorative SVG icons. Providing precise, descriptive `aria-label` attributes (e.g., `aria-label="Share project"`, `aria-label="Close story"`, `aria-label="Like story"`, `aria-label="Share story"`) satisfies WCAG non-text content criteria and guarantees assistive technology screen compatibility without affecting the visual layout or styling of high-fidelity social/media-style components.
**Action:** Always check interactive components in feeds, carousels, and overlays for icon-only triggers. Ensure they carry semantic text or descriptive `aria-label` labels.

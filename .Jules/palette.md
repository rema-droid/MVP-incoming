## 2026-04-29 - Search Accessibility and Usability Standard
**Learning:** Combining a visually hidden label with a conditional clear button and `focus-visible` styles significantly improves the accessibility and usability of search inputs in dark, compact layouts like sidebars. Proper padding is essential to prevent text overlap with the clear icon.
**Action:** Always implement the standardized search pattern (sr-only label, conditional X button, pr-10 padding, and focus-visible ring) for any new or existing search inputs to maintain consistency across the app.

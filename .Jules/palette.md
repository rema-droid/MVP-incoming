## 2025-05-14 - Search Reset & Accessibility Baseline
**Learning:** In apps with multiple search contexts (Sidebar, Mobile), inconsistent or missing 'Clear' actions and accessibility labels create friction for keyboard and screen reader users. Icon-only engagement buttons in social-style feeds are often overlooked during accessibility audits.
**Action:** Always include a 'Clear' button and `aria-label` for search inputs, and ensure icon-only engagement buttons (Like/Share/Comment) have descriptive labels.
